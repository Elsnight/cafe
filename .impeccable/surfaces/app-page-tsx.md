# Homepage

- Target: `app/page.tsx`
- Mode: Persuade
- Audience: Quito residents buying fresh coffee for home delivery.
- Job: Make roast-to-order immediately credible and move the visitor into a complete order.
- Primary action: Configure a pack and continue to the WhatsApp order flow.
- Proof: Fausto is the founder and roaster; Typica; sustainable local sourcing; verified prices and Quito delivery terms.
- Scope: Full responsive sales journey. Preserve pricing, cart state, validation, totals, and WhatsApp submission behavior.
- Anti-goals: Generic luxury coffee, operational dashboard, unapproved identity assets, placeholder photography, invented origin or delivery claims.
- Critique reference: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/roast-ledger.png`

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Every customer order becomes a visible roasting batch, refusing the standard coffee hero followed by interchangeable product cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** A committed field of oat batch paper and charred ink, with roast-orange stamps and muted-green status marks. Condensed workshop display type, readable sans text, monospaced ledger labels, graph rulings, punched holes, stamps, and squared controls make one continuous production artifact. The physical scene is a morning order sheet pinned beside the roaster under clear task light.

**STORY:** The visitor sees that coffee is roasted only after ordering, configures a real pack, understands Fausto’s process and delivery terms, reviews totals, and hands the prepared order to WhatsApp.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A typographic wordmark and compact navigation sit above one oversized active batch sheet. The roast-to-order claim anchors the left; the live three-stage ledger and pack, presentation, and roast controls occupy the center and right. “Agregar al lote” is visible without scrolling.

**FORM:** The Roast Ledger, position 4 in the grounded list. `seed:4617e3f6599831b1`. Code-led; the decision comp remains a critique reference.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
